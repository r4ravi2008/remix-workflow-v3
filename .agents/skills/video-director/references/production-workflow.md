# From direction to production

Use the ordered stages in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for a new video or a substantial redesign. This reference defines the decisions that must be proved before expanding production. For a bounded correction, reuse the accepted treatment and test only the changed risk and its handoffs. A storyboard-only request ends with a storyboard; it requires no generated pilot.

## Make the treatment testable

Put a compact requirement-to-evidence table in the existing brief. Include each material clause, the chosen interpretation, where it will appear, how it will be checked, and its status. Separate user constraints from inherited choices and agent assumptions. An asset count or provider name is implementation evidence, not proof of the requested experience.

For a source/generated hybrid, define what connects the worlds: a shared action, character viewpoint, transformation, reflection, graphic match, or deliberately contrasting montage with a developing motif. Say whether the leads inhabit the new world, appear through compositing/source-derived shots, or stay in source performances while cutaways develop their subjective world. Any of these can work when it fulfills the brief and the edited passage proves the relationship. Alternation alone does not establish that relationship.

Turn style words into shot design. For example, cyberpunk nightlife can involve human activity, technology, material contrast, distinctive city details, lighting and a surreal rule affecting the action. The appropriate choices come from this film. A pink ribbon over empty water proves a neon environment; it does not by itself prove nightlife, a relationship arc or a source-to-surreal transformation.

Keep a few reference frames beside the proposed treatment and compare composition, lighting, texture, subject action and camera grammar. Define how original and generated footage will meet: intended contrast, exposure/color treatment, scale, eyeline, motion direction, and entry/exit. Do not claim a grade joins worlds without inspecting the actual paired shots.

## Plan before filling the timeline

Use the category content map—listened song sections, verified script/speech beats, or deliberate silent action—to give the video an opening promise, development, meaningful turn and ending. Narrative, performance and abstraction are all valid forms. Each scene needs its own starting state, action/change and resulting state. Each shot card names a specific contribution; repeated generic purpose text cannot demonstrate progression.

Allocate scenes to the content progression and turning points. For music, use heard sections and phrases; for spoken stories, use ideas, evidence and pauses. Equal-length blocks need an editorial reason. Separate inherited source camera cuts from new editorial decisions. Onset estimates can support timing; they cannot decide emotional changes or vocal performance suitability.

Review the chronological storyboard/animatic with the intended audio, when present, before expensive coverage. Placeholders may prove order and duration while remaining clearly labeled; they cannot prove identity, generated motion or final polish. Count distinct actions, angles and scene states as well as seconds. A second provider animating a frame from the first provider's shot adds motion footage, but may add no new story coverage.

## Prove one edited passage before expanding

Choose the shortest passage that contains the main risk and a complete editorial idea. A complete musical phrase around 15–30 seconds is useful for music; a narration/graphic passage or the whole short may be appropriate for another category. This is guidance, not a mandatory length. Include the setup, risky operation and landing; add another bounded test if one passage cannot cover a distinct essential risk. Render it from the chosen editor (Resolve by default) with the bound soundtrack/narration when present and the intended presentation. A deliberately silent brief needs visual proof rather than invented audio.

For a source-to-surreal brief, the proof should include original → new world → original when that is the planned grammar. For a character-led new world, include recognizable leads doing the intended action in it. For deliberately separate cutaways, prove their connection to the leads' gaze, action, memory or developing visual motif. Test provider-specific limitations before constructing the full film around them.

Record the passage path/hash, source/song ranges, references, actual methods/ranges reviewed and observations:

| Question | Passing observation |
|---|---|
| Does it realize the brief? | Every material requirement assigned to this passage is perceptible in the render, with a concrete shot/event identified. |
| Does its timing serve the content? | For music, watched/heard evidence confirms phrase shape, energy and performance. For spoken stories, it confirms natural narration, comprehension, graphic dwell and the intended takeaway. For silent work, the sequence and reading rhythm communicate the idea. |
| Are factual implications accurate? | When factual, script, audible claims, graphics and image roles agree with the verified ledger; attribution and uncertainty survive the edit. |
| Do the worlds connect? | The incoming action/image motivates the new shot and its return; lighting, scale and motion support the selected continuity or deliberate contrast. |
| Do the leads work? | Requested identity, proportions, performance and interaction survive actual motion in their intended use. Apply only when characters are part of the test. |
| Is there enough visual development? | Each shot contributes a new action, relationship, viewpoint or motif state; provider variants do not merely repeat one composition. |
| Is the method feasible? | The exact references, generation mode, Resolve technique, usable durations and handles produce the demonstrated result. |

Store this as creative-proof evidence with the storyboard review. It is an editorial decision, not a numeric proxy for taste. A frame-only check can reject a bad face or wrong graphic, but cannot pass temporal performance, musical feel or spoken comprehension. Under autonomous mode, the agent performs these checks and proceeds without inventing a human approval stop. Under user-gated mode, honor the stated boundary and provide the actual passage.

After the proof passes, finalize the storyboard against that approach and generate missing coverage in small scene batches. Review each batch chronologically before expanding. Re-prove a changed central treatment, identity strategy or world-transition method; reuse the evidence when only an unrelated technical setting changes.

## When perception or a required technique is unavailable

Check actual viewing/listening capability at the start. Try supported available tools reasonably and state the precise limitation. Complete independent source verification, references, planning, canvas preparation and technical diagnostics. Where perception is necessary to choose the treatment, keep the diagnostic bounded until the required evidence is obtained. Missing hearing cannot be converted into an accepted song or speech map by waveform analysis. For a deliberately silent brief, use a declared silent category template: retain full watched coverage and replace sound-specific gates with verification of intentional silence. Do this from the brief before fingerprinting/auditing, never to evade an unavailable listening tool for a requested soundtrack.

A provisional diagnostic may consume candidate assets to evaluate them. It does not authorize full-scale downstream generation, an accepted production edit, or a completion claim while its required proof remains pending/failed. If the user explicitly requests a full provisional film despite the disclosed limitation, record that authorization and preserve the failed/pending gate report; the result remains unaccepted. An existing full film can still be handed over for review with its actual limitations. This boundary does not require discarding completed work or withholding the complete context of a proven bounded revision.

When a pilot fails, locate the failure in the references, anchor, actual model input, generation, composition or edit. Repair within the attempt/budget limit and compare. Redesign within the brief when appropriate. If preserving a required character/action/world is infeasible, state that specific blocked requirement and provide a concrete alternative proof. A central change of meaning or requested character treatment requires user direction; a tool failure does not authorize it.

Run [quality-gates.md](quality-gates.md) before promotion. Exit 1 means the requested target has not passed; its report belongs in the decision, not just the final links. Resolve known intent/coherence defects even when playback is unavailable. Provisional evidence can remain pending; an observed mismatch is a failure.

## Review the experience before polishing defects

Review story/idea development, source/generated relationship, category-specific timing and meaningful visual variation first. Then refine framing, transitions, grade, sharpness, text and export defects. Fixing flashes and cropped heads cannot rescue a treatment that never expresses the brief. Apply [revision-and-self-review.md](revision-and-self-review.md) to the exact export and preserve a better predecessor.
